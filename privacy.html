Privacy Policy

Pink Player — Mini for YouTube Music · Last updated 2026-10-20

Summary

  • The extension runs entirely on your own device.
  • It does not create an account. It uses your existing YouTube Music sign-in only to load your own playlists from YouTube Music.
  • It does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifying information.
  • It does not use analytics, telemetry, ads, or tracking pixels.
  • Apart from YouTube Music itself, it makes network requests only to LRCLib, to fetch synchronized lyrics for the track you are playing, and, if you enter a Pink Player Pro license key, to Polar, to check that key.

What data the extension accesses

When you visit music.youtube.com, the extension reads — only from that tab and only while it is open — the following:

  • title, artist, album-art image URL, and duration of the current track,
  • current playback position, paused/playing state, volume, like state,
  • list of upcoming tracks in your YouTube Music queue,
  • the playlists in your YouTube Music library (title, song count, thumbnail, and playlist ID), when you open the Playlists tab.

This data is used solely to display the mini-player UI in the extension popup and is never written to any server or stored beyond the current browser session, except for the few settings listed under "Local storage on your device" below.

To list your playlists, the extension asks YouTube Music for them from inside your own YouTube Music tab, the same way the YouTube Music website loads your library. That request goes only to YouTube Music. To sign it the way YouTube expects, the extension reads YouTube's SAPISID sign-in cookie inside that tab; the cookie is never stored by the extension or sent anywhere other than YouTube Music. The playlist list is kept in memory for about a minute so the tab opens quickly, and is never written to disk.

What data is transmitted off-device

To display synchronized lyrics, the extension sends the track title, artist name, and track duration to https://lrclib.net, a community-run lyrics database. No personal identifiers, IP-based identifiers, or YouTube account information are included in this request. LRCLib operates under its own privacy practices documented on its website.

If you buy Pink Player Pro and enter your license key, the extension sends the key and Pink Player's account ID at Polar to https://api.polar.sh to check that the key is valid. It checks again every few days, so a refunded key stops working. Nothing else is sent. You buy Pro on Polar's checkout page, not in the extension, so Pink Player never sees your payment details or address; Polar (Polar Software, Inc.) processes the order as merchant of record under its privacy policy.

The extension does not transmit anything to any other third party.

Local storage on your device

The extension stores the following inside Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local), which is sandboxed to the extension and never synced to Google:

  • Sleep timer state (mode and target time, while a timer is active).
  • The volume level you last set, so it carries over between tracks.
  • The ID of the browser tab where YouTube Music was last found (a number and a timestamp), so the popup reconnects quickly. No page address or content is stored.
  • Your Pink Player Pro status: whether the Playlists panel is free for you (set when you updated from a version made before Pro) and, if you bought Pro, your license key and when it was last checked. This one item is also kept in chrome.storage.sync, so if you're signed in to Chrome it follows you to your other computers.

That's it. No listening history, no personal identifiers.

Permissions used and why

  • tabs: to locate your open YouTube Music tab so the popup can show current playback state.
  • storage: to remember the items listed under "Local storage on your device" above.
  • alarms: to fire the sleep timer at the requested time.
  • scripting: to load Pink Player into a YouTube Music tab that was already open before the extension was installed or updated (so you don't have to reload it), and to confirm a tab is YouTube Music before sending it commands.
  • Host permission for music.youtube.com: to read player state and send playback commands.
  • Host permission for lrclib.net: to fetch lyrics, as described above.

The Pro license check needs no extra permission: it is an ordinary web request to Polar, which Polar allows from any site.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's behavior changes in a way that affects data handling, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will be bumped.
