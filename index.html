Inside the popup

Three tabs. Everything you reach for.

Up Next

Your queue with album art. Click any song and it plays now.

Lyrics

Synced lyrics that follow the song. Tap a line to jump there.

Playlists

NEW

Every playlist in your library. Pick one and it starts in your YouTube Music tab.

Out in the wild

The real thing, in Chrome and Safari.

Keyboard

Hands stay on the keys.

Shortcuts work in any tab while your browser is in front, even with the popup closed. Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. ⌥ is Alt on Windows.

And the small stuff.

Adaptive color

The player takes its tint from each song’s album art.

Sleep timer

15, 30 or 60 minutes, or the end of the track. Keeps counting with the popup closed.

Pop-out window

A small floating player that stays open while you work.

Light and dark

Follows your system appearance automatically.

Follows the music

With several YouTube Music tabs open, it controls the one that’s playing.

Volume, likes, shuffle

All the controls from the player bar, without switching tabs.
