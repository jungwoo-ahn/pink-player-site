Terms of Sale

Pink Player Pro · Last updated 2026-10-20

What you buy

Pink Player Pro is a one-time purchase of $2.99 (USD). It unlocks the Playlists panel in the Pink Player browser extension for Chrome and Edge, where you can browse your saved YouTube Music playlists and start one from the toolbar. Every other feature of the extension stays free.

  • No subscription. You pay once and keep it.
  • After checkout you get a license key on the confirmation page and by email. Paste it into the extension’s Playlists tab to unlock Pro.
  • The key is for you. Use it on the browsers and computers you use yourself.
  • If you installed Pink Player before version 0.3.0, when Pro was introduced, you keep the Playlists panel for free and don’t need a key.
  • The Mac app with the Safari version is a separate purchase on the Mac App Store. A Pro key doesn’t unlock it, and the Mac app doesn’t include a Pro key for Chrome.

Who sells it

Pink Player and Pink Player Pro are made by Jungwoo Ahn, an independent developer. Orders are processed by Polar (Polar Software, Inc.), which acts as merchant of record: Polar takes the payment, calculates and collects sales tax or VAT for your location, and issues your receipt. Polar’s buyer terms also apply to the purchase. Pink Player Pro is not sold by Google, the Chrome Web Store or YouTube.

Price and taxes

The price is $2.99 USD. Depending on where you live, tax is either included in that price or added at checkout; the checkout page shows the total before you pay. Your bank may convert the charge to your currency.

Refunds

  • If Pro isn’t right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your purchase and you’ll get your money back. No questions asked.
  • Pink Player works with the YouTube Music website, which can change without notice. If a change breaks the Playlists panel and it isn’t working again within 30 days, you can ask for a refund at any time.
  • A refunded key stops unlocking Pro. Your legal rights as a consumer are not affected by these terms.

Contact

For help with a purchase or a refund, reply to your Polar receipt email or use the support address on your receipt. You can also reach the developer through the email on the Chrome Web Store listing.
